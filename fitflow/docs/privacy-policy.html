What this policy covers

FitFlow is a general home-workout app. It provides exercise routines. It does not diagnose disease, treat a medical condition, provide rehabilitation, or act as a medical device.

No FitFlow account or backend

FitFlow does not ask you to create an account and does not operate a FitFlow backend, cloud sync service, advertising service, or analytics service. The app works on your device without a login.

Information stored on your device

FitFlow stores the following on the device, and does not send it to a FitFlow server:

  • fitness profile and preferences
  • capability profile and adaptive progression evidence
  • workout history
  • custom workouts
  • adaptive program progress
  • reminder choices
  • appearance preference

Backups you create

A backup file is created only when you choose Backup & restore. You pick where it is saved. FitFlow does not upload that file. Android automatic backup and device-to-device transfer of FitFlow app data are turned off. A file you already saved remains where you put it until you delete it.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, FitFlow uses notification permission only to show those reminders. After a restart, the boot permission lets FitFlow restore the reminders you scheduled. FitFlow does not use exact alarms.

Voice coaching

Voice coaching uses the text-to-speech service configured on your device. FitFlow does not operate a speech server. The engine selected on the device may be offline or online. FitFlow does not control that provider and does not guarantee that every third-party speech engine stays offline.

Ads and sale of data

FitFlow does not include an advertising SDK or an analytics SDK, and it does not sell FitFlow user data.

Removing the app

Uninstalling FitFlow removes app-owned local state, subject to ordinary operating-system behavior. Backup files you saved outside the app remain until you delete them. There is no FitFlow account to delete.
