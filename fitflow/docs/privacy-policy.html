In one paragraph. Home Workout is an offline home-workout app. It has no user accounts, no backend servers, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you enter is stored on your own device. The released Android build does not even declare the internet permission, so the app is not technically able to send your information anywhere. The only time data leaves the app is when you export a backup file to a folder you pick, or when your device's own text-to-speech engine speaks coaching cues.

1. Who we are

Home Workout is an independent Android application developed and published by DEVELOPER_NAME (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information. It applies to the Home Workout app distributed through Google Play and any directly installed release build with the application ID com.fitflow.fitflow.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give an email address to use the app.
  • We do not operate a server, cloud sync, or online profile for Home Workout.
  • We do not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, attribution SDKs, or any other third-party tracking library.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with anyone.
  • We do not use your data to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, photos, or health-platform data.

Home Workout does not request the internet permission, exact-alarm permission, location, camera, microphone, or broad storage access. Reminders use inexact scheduling, which Android may deliver a little earlier or later depending on battery optimisations.

5. Backups

Home Workout can export your data to a backup file, but only when you choose Profiles & settings → Backup & restore. The file is written wherever you select with the Android file picker, and it is never uploaded by the app. Restoring reads a file you pick and replaces the app's local data after validation.

Android automatic backup and device-to-device transfer for Home Workout app data are disabled (android:allowBackup="false"), so the operating system does not silently copy your workout data to Google Drive or to another device. A backup file you saved stays where you put it until you delete it.

6. Voice coaching and background music

Voice coaching uses the text-to-speech engine already installed and configured on your device. We do not operate a speech service and never receive audio. Depending on the engine you (or your device manufacturer) selected, that engine may process text locally or send it to its own online service under that provider's terms — Home Workout does not control that provider. You can turn voice coaching off in settings, or change or remove the engine in Android settings.

Workout music ships as audio files inside the app and plays offline. There is no streaming, no music account, and no listening analytics. Music is off until you turn it on.

7. Exercise instructions, artwork and third-party components

Exercise instructions, cues and workout logic are authored for Home Workout and stored inside the app. Some exercise animations are adapted from artwork by third-party creators and are used under their licences. The required attribution notices, licence names and licence texts are bundled with the app and can be read offline in Profiles & settings → Exercise artwork credits. That screen is an attribution requirement of those licences, not a tracking or advertising feature.

Home Workout is built with open-source components, including the Flutter engine, the lottie animation library, just_audio and audio_session for audio playback, flutter_tts for speech, flutter_local_notifications and timezone for reminders, shared_preferences for local settings, file_picker for backup files, go_router, and flutter_riverpod. These components run on your device as part of the app.

8. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not share your information because we do not receive it. We may disclose information only if we are legally compelled to — and because the released app transmits nothing to us, we hold no user data to disclose.

9. Retention and deleting your data

  • In-app data lives on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling Home Workout removes the app's private storage, subject to normal Android behaviour.
  • Backup files you exported remain in the location you chose until you delete them yourself. We cannot delete them, because we never see them.
  • There is no server-side copy to request deletion of, and no account to close.

10. Children

Home Workout is a general fitness app intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children — the app collects no information from anyone.

11. Security

Your information stays inside the app's private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps. There is no network transmission to intercept. The main risks are the ones that apply to any device: someone with physical access to an unlocked phone, or a backup file you store in a shared or cloud-synced folder. Keep your device locked and store backups somewhere you trust.

12. Not medical advice

Home Workout provides general fitness workouts and exercise information. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, does not provide rehabilitation or medical advice, and is not a medical device. Speak to a qualified professional before starting a new exercise programme, especially if you are pregnant, recovering from injury, or managing a health condition. Stop if you feel pain or dizziness.
