# Play Health Apps declaration

Prepare this in Play Console. Do not mark the app as having no health features.

## Declaration

**Health and fitness → Activity and Fitness**

FitFlow provides general fitness and workout functionality: exercise routines, capability-based workout selection, reminders, and local progress.

FitFlow:

- does not diagnose disease
- does not treat medical conditions
- is not a medical device
- does not claim rehabilitation functionality
- does not currently access Health Connect
- does not read or write platform health records

The in-app privacy policy states the same boundary. Do not add medical outcome claims to the store listing.
