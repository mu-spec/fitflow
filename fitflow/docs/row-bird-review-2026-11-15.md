# Doorway Row and Bird Dog review

Preview only; not installed. Twenty of 25 animations are approved and
integrated. No APK built.

Original Military Push Ups source paths, colors and part scales are retained.
Rigid joint transforms repose the limbs; original shorts copies use overlap
masks as in the preceding approved exercises. These are locally authored
adaptations under the bundled Lottie Simple License.

Doorway Row keeps palms and feet fixed, leans backward, and pulls the chest
toward a structural jamb. The drawn jamb occludes the original fingertips.
This stylized grip uses the unchanged source hand artwork. Duration: 2.68s.

Bird Dog starts on hands/knees and extends one arm with the opposite leg,
holds briefly, returns and switches sides. The trunk stays fixed and reaches
follow its axis. Both sides use original source parts. Duration: 5.36s, two
repetitions at the existing push-up pace.

Validation: source-path equality, rigid transforms, unchanged installed asset
hashes, targeted Flutter render (40 frames and poster per candidate), visual
contact-sheet review, exact loop closure, stationary row supports and fixed
supporting bird-dog limbs during each side's reach. These are review candidates
and are not claimed independently certified for exercise form.
