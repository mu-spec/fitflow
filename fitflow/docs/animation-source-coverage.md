# Original-creator animation coverage

Research date: 10 November 2026. Scope: FitFlow's 25 active exercises and the Dinh Bui Xuan character style approved by the user.

## Result

Five original motion compositions are already present and previously approved by the user. No additional exact replacement among the remaining 20 was verified in this search. This is a verification gap, not proof that the creator has never made these movements. Indexed searches and public asset pages do not provide an exhaustive inventory of private or unindexed work.

No application assets, exercise definitions or playback code were changed during this audit. Existing locally adapted motions must not be represented as original creator animations or as newly approved by the user.

## Recommended next decision

Keep the five approved assets. Obtain exact existing originals or a quote for the missing movements from the creator, or evaluate another complete licensed collection. Do not alter the exercise catalog merely to fit available artwork. If custom work is chosen, require one approved squat pilot before producing the other movements; use the actual preferred character artwork and review full motion, not just still frames. No creator has been contacted and no paid work has been ordered.
