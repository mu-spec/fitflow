# Launch 25 character demonstrations

Reference artwork: Dinh Bui Xuan / LottieFiles, Lottie Simple License (FL 9.13.21).
The complete terms are bundled in ../real_people/LICENSE-LOTTIE-SIMPLE.md.
Original reference animations: Jumping Jacks, Forward Lunge, Reverse Crunch,
Military Push Ups and Cobras. The latter two were supplied by the app owner.
These five motion compositions are preserved without retargeting.

The other 20 files are locally authored/adapted exercise-specific motions
using the orange-shirt character vector artwork. They are adaptations, not
original animations by or endorsements from Dinh Bui Xuan.
Pose solver: Open Exercise Figures (MIT-0); pose sources and adaptations are
recorded in tools/illustrated_athletes/README.md and tools/orange_character.
Source terms: https://lottiefiles.com/page/license
Posters are rendered from the corresponding motion files.
Planks intentionally demonstrate a held position, not repeated repetitions.

Bodyweight Squat was replaced on 11 November 2026 with the owner-approved
front-facing adaptation of the Jumping Jacks artwork. It uses bent elbows
and hands in front of the chest. Authoring source: tools/squat_sample.

Partial Squat and Chair Sit-to-Stand were replaced on 14 November 2026 with
approved variants of that front-facing squat. Their character artwork is
preserved, with exercise-specific range, timing and stationary chair support.

March in Place and Calf Raise were replaced on 14 November 2026 with approved
front-facing adaptations using the same Jumping Jacks character artwork.
March uses opposite arm/leg movement with hands kept on their respective sides;
Calf Raise uses relaxed arms for the unassisted variation. Authoring source:
tools/squat_sample/build_standing_review.py.

Arm Circles and Step Jack were replaced on 14 November 2026 with approved
front-facing adaptations preserving the source shoulder contours and wrist
connections. Authoring: tools/squat_sample/build_warmup_review.py.
The app applies shared push-up-based demonstration pacing at playback time;
source compositions and workout prescriptions retain their original timing.

Wall Push-Up and Knee Push-Up were replaced on 14 November 2026 with
owner-approved adaptations of Military Push Ups. Original upper-body contours
are retained. Knee Push-Up uses approved continuous, fuller thigh/calf paths;
Wall Push-Up keeps the shorts panels aligned with the pelvis. Both have fixed
support contacts and a 2.68-second repetition. Authoring:
tools/squat_sample/build_press_review.py.

Hip Hinge was replaced on 15 November 2026 with the owner-approved adaptation
preserving original Military Push Ups contours and part sizes. Original parts
are repositioned for the hands-on-hips hinge with a 2.68-second repetition.
Authoring: tools/squat_sample/build_hinge_review.py.

Glute Bridge was replaced on 15 November 2026 with the owner-approved
adaptation of Military Push Ups. Original parts are reposed with rigid
transforms; overlapping masks on copies of the original shorts connect the
pelvis and thighs. Arms use the original bent-arm artwork. The head and
support contacts remain stationary. Authoring:
tools/squat_sample/build_bridge_review.py.

Reverse Lunge and Pike Push-Up were replaced on 15 November 2026 with
owner-approved Military Push Ups adaptations. Original body-part paths are
reposed, with masks on copies of the shorts connecting the hip/thigh overlaps.
Reverse Lunge alternates sides over 5.36 seconds; Pike Push-Up uses a 2.68-second
repetition. Authoring: tools/squat_sample/build_next_pair_review.py.

Knee Plank and Forearm Plank were replaced on 15 November 2026 with
owner-approved Military Push Ups adaptations. Original shoulder, upper-arm
and forearm contours use the authored bent-arm pose at frame 31. Original
hands are reposed at the wrists; Knee Plank retains the approved fuller
supported-knee contours. Both use a 2.68-second breathing loop with stationary
support contacts. Authoring: tools/squat_sample/build_plank_review.py.


2026-11-16: Approved Doorway Row and Bird Dog refinements integrated from original Military Push Ups artwork. Bird Dog shoe orientation follows the ankle. Source attribution/license unchanged.

Dead Bug, Cat-Cow and Reverse Snow Angel were replaced on 16 November 2026 with
the owner-approved final batch. All three reuse the original Military Push Ups
part artwork with rigid limb transforms: Dead Bug alternates opposite arm/leg
reaches over a 5.36-second dual repetition, Cat-Cow uses a 2.68-second loop and
bends the original shirt path between fixed hip and shoulder endpoints to show
spinal motion (its shirt path is therefore not byte-identical to the source),
and Reverse Snow Angel uses the approved oblique side projection for the prone
arm sweep over 2.68 seconds. Authoring: tools/squat_sample/build_final_three_review.py.
Publication: tools/squat_sample/publish_final_three.py (only these six files
changed; the other 22 motion/poster pairs were hash-verified unchanged).
Source attribution and license are unchanged.
